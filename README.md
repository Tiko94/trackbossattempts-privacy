# Privacy Policy for TrackBossAttempts
*Last Updated: November 2, 2026*

TrackBossAttempts (“the Application”) is designed with user privacy as a primary feature. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data storage, media handling, and iCloud synchronization when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Data Storage and iCloud Synchronization
TrackBossAttempts operates using local device storage paired with Apple's secure **iCloud (CloudKit)** infrastructure:
* **Local Device Storage:** All data created within the Application—including game titles, boss names, attempt counts, status selections, notes, and uploaded boss photos—is stored locally on your device using iOS SwiftData persistence.
* **iCloud Synchronization:** Starting in Version 1.3, the Application utilizes Apple's CloudKit service to automatically back up and synchronize your tracking data across your personal iOS and iPadOS devices logged into the same Apple ID.
* **Data Ownership & Access:** Your synchronized data resides strictly within your personal, private iCloud container. The developer does not operate external servers, cannot access, view, or sell your data, and does not harvest personal identifiers.

## 2. Media Access (Photos & Images)
If you choose to upload a boss photo, the Application utilizes standard iOS system pickers (`PhotosPicker`). The Application does not request full photo library permissions and only accesses the specific images you explicitly select. These images remain stored locally on your device and within your private iCloud backup.

## 3. Third-Party Services & Tracking
The Application does not incorporate any third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs), advertising networks, tracking tools, or external analytics frameworks. Apple CloudKit is utilized strictly for background data synchronization. No personal data or metrics are ever sent to third-party entities or external developer servers.

## 4. App Extensions & Widgets
Data displayed in Home Screen or Lock Screen widgets utilizes local iOS App Groups to sync metrics directly between the main app container and the widget extension on your device. No internet connection or remote server is involved in this process.

## 5. Data Control and Deletion
Since all data is stored within your local storage and personal iCloud account, you maintain full control over your information at all times. You can edit or delete individual boss entries directly within the Application, manage your iCloud app data through your iOS System Settings, or clear all data permanently by uninstalling the Application from your device.
