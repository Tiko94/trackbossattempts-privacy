    <h1>Privacy Policy for TrackBossAttempts</h1>
    <p class="last-updated">Last updated: November 2, 2026</p>

    <p>TrackBossAttempts ("the App") is designed with a privacy-first approach. We believe your gaming data, attempts, and notes belong entirely to you.</p>

    <h2>1. Data Storage and iCloud Synchronization</h2>
    <p>TrackBossAttempts operates using local storage on your device and Apple's <strong>iCloud (CloudKit)</strong> service:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Local Device Storage:</strong> Your boss attempt counts, game titles, and notes are stored locally on your device.</li>
        <li><strong>iCloud Sync:</strong> Starting in Version 1.3, the App utilizes Apple’s CloudKit infrastructure to automatically back up and synchronize your data across your personal iOS and iPadOS devices.</li>
        <li><strong>Data Control:</strong> Your data is stored within your private, personal iCloud container. The developer does not operate external servers, cannot view, access, store, or sell your data, and does not track your identity.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>2. Image Generation & Sharing (Victory Cards)</h2>
    <p>The App includes a feature to render and export "Victory Share Cards" as images:</p>
    <ul>
        <li>All card rendering takes place strictly on your local device.</li>
        <li>When you choose to share a card, the image is passed directly to the standard iOS system Share Sheet.</li>
        <li>No shared images, media, or associated metadata are sent to external third-party servers.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>3. Third-Party Analytics and Tracking</h2>
    <p>TrackBossAttempts does <strong>not</strong> collect personal identifier data, tracking cookies, or third-party behavioral analytics. We do not integrate third-party advertising frameworks or tracking SDKs.</p>

    <h2>4. Children’s Privacy</h2>
    <p>Because the App does not collect or transmit personal information, it is safe for users of all ages and fully compliant with privacy standards regarding children's data.</p>

    <h2>5. Changes to This Privacy Policy</h2>
    <p>We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new features or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with an updated revision date.</p>

    <h2>6. Contact Us</h2>
    <p>If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out via GitHub or your preferred app support contact.</p>
