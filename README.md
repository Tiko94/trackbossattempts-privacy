# Privacy Policy for TrackBossAttempts

**Last Updated:** September 23, 2026

TrackBossAttempts ("the Application") is designed with user privacy as a primary feature. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection and storage when you use our mobile application.

---

## 1. Data Collection and Storage
TrackBossAttempts **does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data, usage analytics, or device identifiers**.

All data created or modified within the Application—including game titles, boss names, attempt counts, status selections, notes, and uploaded boss photos—is stored strictly on your local device using iOS SwiftData persistence. 

## 2. Media Access (Photos & Images)
If you choose to upload a boss photo, the Application utilizes standard iOS system pickers (`PhotosPicker`). The Application does not request full photo library permissions and only accesses the specific images you explicitly select. These images remain stored locally on your device.

## 3. Third-Party Services & Tracking
The Application does not incorporate any third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs), advertising networks, tracking tools, or external analytics frameworks. No data is ever sent to external servers or third-party entities.

## 4. App Extensions & Widgets
Data displayed in Home Screen or Lock Screen widgets utilizes local iOS App Groups to sync metrics directly between the main app container and the widget extension on your device. No internet connection or remote server is involved in this process.

## 5. Data Control and Deletion
Since all data is stored locally on your device, you maintain full control over your information at all times. You can edit or delete individual boss entries directly within the Application, or clear all data permanently by uninstalling the Application from your device.
